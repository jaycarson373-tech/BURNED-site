ELIGIBILITY RULESTHE ENTRY HAS TO BE REAL.View rules
VERIFIED BUY
Creates tracked units and cost basis. Later verified buys update weighted average entry.
SELL
Any sell excludes the wallet from the current 15-minute epoch.
SEND
An outgoing transfer excludes the wallet and consumes verified units first.
RECEIVE
Incoming transfers create no basis and no eligible units.
SNAPSHOT AND WEIGHT
Eligible units must be below entry with no sell or send in the epoch. Rewards are weighted by eligible unrealized loss, subject to any wallet cap and rounding.
