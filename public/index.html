HOW IT WORKS

A reward loop built for the red.

Entry, eligibility, and rewards come from finalized on-chain activity in the configured BURNED / EMBER pool.

01

ESTABLISH ENTRY

Buy with EMBER.

Only verified pool buys establish eligible units and weighted average entry. Tokens received by transfer carry no entry value.

02

ENTER THE ZONE

Stay below entry.

At the epoch cutoff, retained verified buys are compared with the higher of closing pool price and the 15-minute average.

03

RECEIVE EMBER

Share the fees.

Available claimed EMBER fees are divided by measured unrealized loss, capped by that loss and protected from overspending.

ELIGIBILITY ENGINE

Transfers cannot fake an entry.

The tracker follows finalized transactions from launch. A wallet can qualify only with units it bought through an approved pool and still holds at the cutoff.
