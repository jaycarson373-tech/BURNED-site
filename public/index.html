Only verified buys create an eligible entry. Transferred tokens do not qualify. Selling or sending tokens during an epoch excludes your wallet from that round. Receiving a transfer adds no eligible tokens and does not penalize your own buys. Rewards depend on the fees available. To reduce price manipulation, each round uses the higher of the closing pool price and its 15-minute average.
